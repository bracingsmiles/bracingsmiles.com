FAQ

Common questions

At what age should my child first see an orthodontist?

An early check around age 7 helps spot jaw or bite problems while they are easiest to guide, often with functional or myofunctional therapy.

Braces or clear aligners: which is right for me?

It depends on your bite, the complexity of the case and your lifestyle. Both options are explained at your consultation.

Am I too old for braces?

No. Healthy teeth can be moved at any age, and adult orthodontics offers discreet options such as ceramic braces and aligners.

How long does treatment take?

Most cases take 12–24 months. Your plan includes a clear timeline before you start.

Do you treat cleft lip and palate patients?

Yes. Cleft care, including Naso-Alveolar Moulding (NAM) for infants, is one of Dr. Jennifer's specialities.
