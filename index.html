About

Meet Dr. Jennifer

Dr. Jennifer Mascarenhas holds a Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics. She treats crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites and crossbites, and plans each case for long-term stability as well as appearance.

Patients describe her approach as calm, clear and unhurried. Every option, timeline and cost is explained before treatment begins.

How I can help

Metal braces

Reliable and efficient. The proven choice for complex corrections at any age.

Ceramic braces

Clear aligners

Removable, nearly invisible trays for mild to moderate alignment.

Early orthodontics

Guiding jaw growth in children to prevent bigger problems later.

Adult orthodontics

It's never too late. Discreet options that fit a working life.

Retainers
