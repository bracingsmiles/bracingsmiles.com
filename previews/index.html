FAQ

Common questions

At what age should my child first see an orthodontist?

An early check around age 7 helps spot jaw or bite issues while they're easiest to guide.

Braces or clear aligners: which is right for me?

It depends on your bite and lifestyle. We'll go through both options at your consultation.

How long does treatment take?

Most cases take 12–24 months; your plan will include a clear timeline.

Do braces hurt?

Expect mild soreness for a few days after adjustments, which is easily managed.
